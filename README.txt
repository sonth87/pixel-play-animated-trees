12 frames a second.
Back to front: ground, plant, grass, leaves.
Play shake and fall together. Shake ends on idle.
The pivot is the foot of the trunk.
The wind blows to the right.
The cherry fall is two files. Play them in order.


BONUS

Open play/play.html in a browser. The trees in there are live:
tap one, drag a branch, send a storm.
play/pixel-play.js puts them on a web page of your own.
